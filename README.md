# ESS Information Encounters and Immigration Attitudes

## Reproducible Python workflow for European Social Survey analysis of information encounters, human values, and immigration attitudes among European managers

This repository contains the reproducible Python workflow associated with the article:

**Ahmadinia, H.**  
*Shaping Immigration Attitudes: The Role of Human Values, Media Engagement and Sociopolitical Events Among European Managers.*  
**Nordic Journal of Migration Research**, 16(1), Article 6, 1–25.  
https://doi.org/10.33134/njmr.964

The workflow uses European Social Survey (ESS) data from rounds 8–11 to examine how human values and information encounters through general internet use and political-news consumption are associated with attitudes towards immigration, with particular attention to European managers and other workers as a comparison group.

---

## Study overview

The analysis combines four rounds of the European Social Survey:

| ESS round | Study year |
|---|---:|
| ESS 8 | 2016 |
| ESS 9 | 2018 |
| ESS 10 | 2020 |
| ESS 11 | 2023 |

The final analytical sample contains:

- **117,752 respondents**
- **9,263 European managers**
- **108,489 other European workers**
- **18 European countries**
- **4 ESS rounds**
- **72 country-year combinations**

The workflow examines:

- attitudes towards immigration;
- self-transcendence values;
- conservation values;
- general internet use;
- political and current-affairs news consumption;
- managerial status;
- age;
- gender;
- educational attainment;
- income adequacy;
- religiosity; and
- political orientation.

The statistical analysis uses multilevel linear models to examine individual-, occupational-, country-, and time-related variation in immigration attitudes.
